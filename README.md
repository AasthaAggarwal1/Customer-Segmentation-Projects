Customer Segmentation & RFM Analysis | Power BI

An interactive Customer Segmentation and RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis dashboard built using Power BI to analyze customer purchasing behavior, identify high-value customer segments, and uncover retention and revenue opportunities.

📊 Project Overview

This project transforms transactional sales data into actionable customer insights using RFM analysis. Customers are segmented based on their purchasing behavior into five groups:

🏆 Champions
💙 Loyal Customers
🌱 Potential Loyalists
⚠️ At Risk
🔔 Needs Attention

The dashboard provides an executive-level overview as well as detailed RFM and customer-segment analysis.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze customer purchasing behavior
Segment customers using RFM methodology
Identify high-value customers
Understand revenue contribution by customer segment
Identify customers at risk of churn
Compare customer frequency, recency, and monetary value
Provide business-oriented insights for customer retention and growth

💡 Key Insights

The analysis highlights a strong concentration of revenue among high-value customers. In particular, the Champions segment contributes approximately 78% of total revenue, despite representing only around 1.2K customers.

The RFM analysis also shows a clear behavioral pattern:

Higher-value segments tend to have higher purchase frequency, higher monetary value, and lower recency, indicating stronger and more recent customer engagement.

These insights can support strategies such as:

Champions → Retain & reward
Loyal Customers → Upsell & cross-sell
Potential Loyalists → Nurture & convert
At Risk → Re-engage
Needs Attention → Reactivate
